2026 cumulative data-update models

Executed data introduction in the draft SAFE assessment

Author

Jim Ianelli

Published

October 14, 2026

Modified

October 25, 2026

The 2026 draft SAFE follows the completed cumulative data updates below. Each stage inherits the preceding stage’s inputs. The accepted-model comparison uses Model 23.0 in ADMB; the subsequent Model 23.2 bridge and Model 26.0 Rceattle fit are structural/platform comparisons after the data updates. Numerical completion, selection of a working fit and scientific acceptance retain separate meanings.

The original 0–0.9 plan was revised during implementation. The provisional BTS split and separate fixed-calibration AVO contrast were superseded by the executed sequence. This page records the current selected attempts, including later composition-weight refreshes, as reviewed on 25 October 2026.

The selected cumulative lineage after the spawning correction is fishery CAA → BTS → ATS → AVO. The first ATS/AVO fits preceded the final fishery sample-size revisions in calendar time; the later ATS/AVO refreshes inherit those revised upstream inputs. The attempt suffixes preserve this history rather than renumbering previous fitted runs.

Applied conventions are explicit:

  • The model endpoint is 2026; the 2026 catch input is a forecast of 1.270 million t. Fishery age observations end in 2025.
  • Missing survey weights use finite positive temporal means within the same age, rather than means pooled across ages. BTS 2026 input effective N is 130.789474; ATS 2026 composition N is 94. These are likelihood weights, separate from raw fish counts.
  • ATS biomass errors have arithmetic mean CV 20% over the 20 survey years 1994–2026, with the recorded 2000 exception of 45%. The age-1 index uses log-scale sigma 1. The 2020 USV biomass is an observation; its inherited age-1 substitute and lightly weighted composition retain their distinct qualification.
  • AVO uses 20 observations in 2006–2026, excluding 2020. The 23% calibration preserves relative annual assessment-CV variation with a common scaling factor. Raw sampling errors retain their source meaning.

Keep the endpoints distinct

Fishery catch-at-age ends in 2025; survey observations follow their actual sampling years. BTS and provisional ATS age products extend through 2026. The 2026 ATS conversion uses BTS FT-NIRS length/age information followed by the BTS paired conversion to traditional microscope-age consistency. Its provisional survey composition and weights are expected to be updated with survey age-composition data in 2027. The applied provisional fishery age basis, conversion limitations and uncertainty in supplied weight schedules remain scientific-review items.

The subsequent Model 23.2 bridge uses the same final updated observations under its established structural alignment, including supplied weights. The selected Rceattle execution retains the established Model 26.0 structure and updated observations. Saved run identifiers use 26.2; the draft SAFE displays Model 26.0. Changing the display label created no additional fit. The older through-2024 Model 26.0 development fit remains a historical reference.

The Model 23.2 bridge supports annual-trajectory comparison; classified zero-variance management reports are excluded from advice. Adjacent changes depend on this cumulative order. The coupled BTS release and ATS/AVO observation/error updates leave individual component effects combined. The optional fishery-2024-only, BTS-2025-only and fixed-2024-calibration AVO contrasts remain unrun attribution proposals, rather than missing stages of the completed sequence.

Matched retrospectives, observation-error self-tests and conditional posterior checks are separate evaluation work. The later proper-prior posterior sensitivity preserves the production incremental fits and does not replace the selected working assessment. Its posterior median is not the point estimate in the table above. Follow the annual model checks and projection handoff for the corresponding workflow.

Source and open decision

This execution record reconciles the revised October sequence against the selected native reports and run records, with input/output identity and numerical checks verified on 25 October 2026. The original 0.5/0.6 BTS split, 0.8 fixed-calibration AVO step and 0.9 25%-CV contrast describe superseded plans; they are not the fitted final path. The applied AVO mean CV is 23%.

Scientific review remains open for provisional age products and their conversion assumptions, inherited ATS age substitutes, composition weighting, fixed-weight uncertainty and remaining selectivity/data-influence tests. Francis weighting diagnostics have been calculated but are not applied. Projection selectivity alternatives remain under evaluation; final harvest advice and assessment adoption require their own review decisions. This page publishes the scoped workflow and aggregate execution account; the full draft SAFE, raw data, fitted objects and unrelated diagnostic details remain with the owning assessment workflow.
